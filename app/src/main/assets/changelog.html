Streamwise Changelog

Custom ROI & Trial Pricing Updates

August 2026
  • FeatureCustom Trial Prices: You can now enter custom prices (e.g., .99 promos) in the Subs settings so your ROI calculations use your real costs.
  • FeatureAuto-Expiring Trials: Set the days until a trial ends; Streamwise will automatically deactivate the service when the time is up to keep your ROI accurate.
  • UI/UXBudget Tracker: The ROI tab now acts as a monthly burn rate tracker, calling out "Cancel Candidates" that fall below the 3 hours/mo benchmark.
  • UI/UXPotential Savings: Identifies underutilized subscriptions and displays how much you could save by pausing them.
  • FixWatchlist Filters: Fixed a bug where titles with no streaming provider data were improperly bypassing the "My Services" filter.
  • FeatureIn-App Documentation: Added this User Guide and Changelog directly to the app!

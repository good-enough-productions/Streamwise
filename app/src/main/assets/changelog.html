v1.5.6 September 2026

UI Streamlining, Cinephile Auteur Analytics & Subscription Rotation Advisor

  • Watched Vault Header Rework (Resolves #16): Moved the bulky top card into the scrollable list (LazyColumn and LazyVerticalGrid) with a collapsible summary bar (🎬 1,130 Films • ~2,071h • ★ 7.2 • ~7.0/mo). Restored 90%+ vertical screen space for movie browsing, accompanied by a slim sticky search, sort, and view mode bar.
  • "Ah Hah" Cinephile Analytics (Resolves #17):
    • Top Directors & Auteurs: Exact title matching across 20+ acclaimed director filmographies (Coen Brothers, Nolan, Scorsese, Fincher, Tarantino, Ridley Scott, Cameron, Spielberg, PTA, Kubrick, Linklater, Villeneuve) with film counts, average ratings, and sample chips.
    • Top Actors & Screen Presence: Curated performer tracking surfacing top stars (Tom Cruise, Matt Damon, Leonardo DiCaprio, Brad Pitt, Robert De Niro, Christian Bale, Margot Robbie, Al Pacino, Emma Stone, Ryan Gosling).
    • Cinephile Blind Spots & Gaps: Cross-references vault gaps (<3% representation in Golden Age Cinema, 1970s New Hollywood, Westerns, Documentaries) against your 1,100+ Watchlist queue to surface queued classics ready to explore.
  • Subscription Expected Value & Rotation Advisor (Resolves #15): In My Services, calculates your actual monthly watch velocity (~7.0 films/mo) and effective cost per film. Features the Single-Service Rotation Advisor demonstrating ~$49/mo ($588/yr) savings by subscribing to 1 service at a time while highlighting months of watchlist backlog ready on that platform.

v1.5.5 September 2026

Data Integrity Hardening, Strict TMDB Gating & Filter Fixes

  • Data Integrity Purge & Non-Movie Blacklist: Scrubbed 470 non-movie podcast discussions (drafts, auctions, mailbags, Oscar predictions, hall of fame shows) out of the Watchlist. Added MediaTitleSanitizer with strict regex filtering and candidate title normalization.
  • Strict TMDB Gating: Enforced mandatory TMDB validation on podcast syncs and imports. Only verified feature films and series are admitted to the Watchlist.
  • Room v9 Database Migration & Performance: Upgraded Room database schema to v9 (adding releaseDate TEXT column in v8, and automated cursor-window-size cleanup and text column trimming in v9). Configured 50MB CursorWindow allocation in StreamApp to permanently avoid cursor window limits.
  • Podcast Filter Fix (Resolves #13): Bypassed filterOnlyMyServices whenever a podcast filter or platform filter is active, allowing cinema fans to explore all films covered on The Rewatchables, The Big Picture, Unspooled, How Did This Get Made?, and What Went Wrong regardless of personal streaming subscriptions.
  • Instant TMDB Match Resolution (Resolves #14): Strips podcast prefixes (e.g., "The Re-", "(Re)", guest names, episode numbers) and matches candidates against TMDB before display.

v1.5.4 September 2026

Actor Age at Release & Cinephile Viewing Analytics Hub

  • Actor Age at Release (from actor-age): The Movie Details Bottom Sheet now features a horizontal Cast & Age scrollbar. For every featured cast member, Streamwise displays their profile photo, character name, and dynamically computes their exact age at the film's release date (e.g. 🎂 Age 38 at release) with deceased indicators (🕊️ (Deceased)) where applicable. Powered by parallel TMDB person details lookups with thread-safe in-memory caching.
  • Cinephile Viewing Analytics & Eras (from movies-dataset): The Watched Vault now features an interactive Analytics command center. Tap the Analytics banner or top action icon to inspect:
    • 4 Cinephile KPIs: Total Films Watched, Total Screen Time (Hours & Days), Average Rating, and Peak Cinema Era.
    • Cinema Eras & Decades Breakdown: Visual progress bars and counts across 2020s, 2010s, 2000s, 1990s, 1980s, 1970s, and Pre-1970s classic cinema with 1-tap filtering.
    • Genre Distribution & Ratings: Favorite genres sorted by logged frequency alongside your average star rating for each genre.
    • Streaming Service Used Breakdown: Track where you actually watched each film (Netflix, Max, Criterion, Theatrical, etc.) with 1-tap service tagging in Movie Details and vault filtering.
    • Letterboxd Rating Distribution: 5-tier star rating histogram (9-10★ Masterpieces down to 0.5-2.9★).
    • Viewing Rhythm Milestone: Highlights your all-time most active viewing month and year-over-year watch volumes.
  • Active Filters Ribbon & Quick Clear: Added 1-tap dismissible filter chips and a "Clear All" action for active Era and Service filters in the Watched Vault.

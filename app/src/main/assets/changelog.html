Streamwise Changelog

v8.5: High-Speed Subnet TV Discovery & Interactive Scan UI

September 2026
  • FeatureHybrid Subnet Port Sweep: Upgraded TV discovery from fragile SSDP multicast (which most home mesh routers block) to a high-speed concurrent TCP subnet sweep (ports 5555, 8008, 8009, 8998). Automatically detects and binds Danny's Fire TV (192.168.86.202) and Google Cast displays in under 200ms.
  • UI/UXInteractive Wi-Fi Scan Feedback: The TV Connect modal now displays real-time progress indicators: animated spinning indicator, progress bar, and "Scanning Wi-Fi subnet..." status messages so you always know scanning is actively running.
  • UI/UXDiscovered Device Badges: Devices are rendered with distinct device icons, exact friendly names, IPs, and active selection indicators (Active ✓).

v8.4: TMDB Runtime Ingestion, Duration Filtering & Deep-Link Audit

September 2026
  • FixDuration Filtering (<90m, <120m): Integrated TMDB Movie Details API (/movie/{id}) to ingest exact runtimes and release dates. Fixed filter predicate so movies over 90m and 120m are properly excluded when chips are active.
  • FeatureDuration Sorting: Added "Shortest Duration" option to the queue sorting dropdown to organize your queue from quickest to longest films.
  • UI/UXActive Filter Summary Bar: Displays exact count of matching movies when filters are active, along with a 1-tap "Clear all filters" action.
  • FeatureExpanded Provider Deep-Links: Audited and updated FireTvRelay.kt with direct app/web deep-links for Apple TV+, Criterion Channel, Peacock, Paramount+, Pluto TV, Freevee, MUBI, Shudder, Starz, and BritBox.
  • UI/UXMovie Details Runtime Badge: Surfaced release year and runtime duration (e.g. 2018 · 89 min) in the Movie Details sheet.

v8.3: Instant Undo Deletion & Universal TV Cast Hub

September 2026
  • FixInstant Deletion Recovery & Malevolent Restored: Restored accidentally deleted film Malevolent (2018) to the Watchlist queue. Added an animated Undo banner at the top of the Watchlist and an interactive UNDO action to bottom Snackbars.

v8.1: Universal TV Auto-Discovery, Letterboxd Sync & Master Ledger

September 2026
  • FeatureUniversal TV Auto-Discovery: Uses Wi-Fi SSDP/DIAL discovery to automatically identify Fire TVs and Smart TVs on your subnet and fetch friendly names. Zero manual IP entry required.
  • FeatureAdaptive "Watch Now" Sheet: Context-aware playback sheet that dynamically routes to your discovered TV at home, or falls back to your phone if away.
  • FeatureFire TV Watch Session Tracking: Automatically times playback on your TV and triggers a check-in upon completion to log your viewing and calculate ROI.
  • FeatureQuick Watched Logger: Precision logging modal with 0.5–5.0 star selector, rewatch indicator, runtime, notes, and subscription attribution.
  • FeatureLetterboxd CSV Export: 1-tap export in the Vault generating Downloads/letterboxd_import.csv for direct import into Letterboxd.
  • Feature$0/mo Master Google Sheet Ledger: Serverless Google Apps Script webhook deployed and baked into app settings. Two-way sync with zero copy-pasting.
  • Feature"Do It Now" Feedback FAB: Floating bug and feature reporting button on every screen with Compose screenshot capture, system telemetry, and GitHub issue filing.
  • DatabaseRoom Schema v8: Added ratings, rewatches, Letterboxd URIs, sync status, runtimes, and release years.

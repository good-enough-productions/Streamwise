Streamwise Release Changelog

v1.5.1 September 2026

Gemini Spark Podcast Sync & Feedback Polish

  • Gemini Spark Podcast Tracker Integration: Added 1-tap sync button in both Explore (Podcasts sub-tab) and Settings (Profile & Letterboxd) to pull new movie recommendations from your personal Google Sheet tracker. New films are added to your Watchlist tagged with origin details (podcast name, episode, air date, recommender), and previously seen movies in your Watched Vault are enriched with discussion notes.
  • TMDB Sync Loop Fix: Resolved repetitive "Syncing 7 titles" toast on every app launch. Movies with no US streaming availability now persist a none sentinel, and the sync filter no longer redundantly re-queries already enriched titles.
  • Backlog-First Feedback Polish: Feedback dialog now defaults to submitting directly to the project backlog, displaying "Submitting to backlog..." with accurate toasts. Jules autonomous AI assignment is strictly opt-in via checkbox.

v1.5.0 September 2026

Explore UI/UX Redesign & Comprehensive Letterboxd Pipeline

  • Modular 4-Subtab Explore Hub: Rebuilt the Explore tab to eliminate excessive scrolling. Content is now partitioned into 4 focused sub-views:
    • ✨ AI & Taste: Dynamic Cinephile Taste Matrix, Gemini Pro Cinephile Synthesis with core themes and auteur ties, tailored picks with 1-tap + Watchlist, and quick prompts.
    • 💬 Olivia AI: Dedicated fullscreen conversational cinema assistant with persistent badge counter.
    • 🎙️ Podcasts Hub: Dedicated show filter chips (What Went Wrong, The Rewatchables, The Big Picture, Blank Check, How Did This Get Made?), episode counts, instant audio listening links, and 💬 Ask Olivia integration.
    • 📰 Film News Hub: Categorized industry dispatches (Box Office, Festivals, Auteurs, Streaming) with 1-tap 💬 Discuss with Olivia.
  • Letterboxd Comprehensive Sync & Dual-Way Pipeline:
    • Default username set to scriptedmind.
    • Native CSV & ZIP File Import: Ingest watchlist.csv, watched.csv, diary.csv, ratings.csv, or full Letterboxd data export ZIPs (letterboxd-*-data.zip) with smart deduplication against local Room database.
    • Two-Way Export for Letterboxd: Export watched movies logged in Streamwise directly into Letterboxd's standard import format (Title,Year,Rating10,WatchedDate) via Android share sheet for 1-tap upload to letterboxd.com/import/.
    • Direct Web Actions: 1-tap web action buttons in Settings to download Watchlist CSV, export account data, or open Letterboxd importer.
    • Google Apps Script Portal: StreamwiseLetterboxdSync.gs serverless web app archiving backups in Google Drive (Active Builds / Streamwise / Backups & Exports) and updating Google Sheets ledger.

v1.4.0 September 2026

Added & Unified Hub

  • Watchlist Title Count Clarity: Live title counts prominently displayed across the Top App Bar (Watchlist • 12 Titles), Bottom Navigation badges (Badge { 12 }), and Sticky Header summary (Showing 12 of 12 titles).
  • Podcasts & Media Mentions Filter: Added a dedicated podcast section in the Advanced Filters sheet featuring What Went Wrong, The Rewatchables, The Big Picture, Blank Check, and How Did This Get Made? with an optional sub-filter for "Featured Discussion / Main Topic Only" vs general mentions.
  • Service Details & Live Deal Finder: Clickable provider cards in My Services open a detailed bottom sheet displaying subscription tenure, renewal billing day, custom plan name, ROI $/hr metrics, and 1-tap live deal queries across top deal aggregators (The Streamable Deals, Slickdeals, Doctor of Credit, and Google Deals).
  • Live Letterboxd RSS Sync: Pull real-time diary entries directly from letterboxd.com/{username}/rss/ without an API key, automatically adding newly logged movies, star ratings, and watch dates to your local Watched Vault with an interactive progress dialog.
  • 4-Tab Modern Settings & Hub: Unified Settings into 4 organized tabs: Profile & Letterboxd, Streaming Services (19), Guides & Docs, and OTA Updates & System.
  • In-App GitHub OTA Auto-Updater: Bundled GitHubUpdateManager with FileProvider to check GitHub Releases, display changelogs, download APK updates with live progress bars, and hand off directly to Android's PackageInstaller.
  • In-App User Guide & Changelog Viewers: Built-in web dialogs render rich documentation directly inside the app.
  • Expanded 19-Provider Coverage: Comprehensive support for AMC+, Kanopy, and Hoopla alongside Netflix, Hulu, Max, Disney+, Prime Video, Apple TV+, Paramount+, Peacock, Criterion Channel, MUBI, Shudder, Starz, BritBox, Tubi, Freevee, and Pluto TV.

v1.3.1 September 2026

Enhanced & Streamlined

  • Natural Scrolling Spotlight & Sticky Search: The Spotlight "Ready to Stream" carousel now scrolls naturally with the feed. When scrolling down, Spotlight disappears cleanly while Search and Filter ribbon stick firmly to the top. Scrolling back to the top immediately restores Spotlight.

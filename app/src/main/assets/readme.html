Streamwise: Founder command Center v6

Welcome, Founder. This manual documents the technical architecture, operational flows, and future roadmap for your private cinematic legacy vault.

1. Core Navigation

  • Watchlist: Your active backlog. Includes "Vibe Match" scores from Gemma 4.
  • Watched (The Vault): Your history of 400+ titles. Sorted by watch timeline.
  • ROI Stats: Financial analytics tracking which subscriptions are providing the most value based on your watch hours.

2. Technical Architecture (The ELI5)

How it communicates:

Your phone behaves like a Remote Terminal. When it needs to "think" about a movie, it sends a request over your local Wi-Fi to 192.168.86.217 (your laptop). Your laptop runs Ollama, which hosts Gemma 4. No data ever leaves your house.

Where data lives:

The app uses a Room (SQLite) database directly on your phone. This is your "source of truth."

  • Local Cache: Posters and metadata from TMDB.
  • Research Vault: The monospaced research cards generated by Gemma.
  • Seed Data: Initialized from watchlist.csv and watched_history.csv in the assets folder.

3. Local AI: Gemma 4 Capabilities

Gemma 4 acts as your Cinematic Research Agent (Olivia). Current capabilities:

  • Personal Relevance: Analyzes your last 15 watched movies to score new titles.
  • Trend Identification: Finds connecting themes (e.g., "Techno-Paranoia", "2000s Cyberpunk").
  • Contextual Synthesis: Generates structured YAML frontmatter for each movie.

Future boundaries:

With Synthesis 3.0, we can enable "Function Calling." This means you could say "Add The Matrix to my list" and the app would interpret Gemma's response to actually update your database.

🎬 Streamwise User Guide

Streamwise is your intelligent streaming companion and subscription optimizer. It unifies your Watchlist across every service, routes playback directly to your Smart TV, logs your viewing history, and uses a forward-looking recommendation engine to save you money every month.

0. 🚀 First-Run Onboarding & Streamwise Pro Tiers

Streamwise provides immediate value upon first opening the app:

  • 3-Step Onboarding Wizard: Select your active streaming services, optionally connect your Letterboxd username, and instantly view your current monthly subscription spend alongside an immediate "Free to Me" queue.
  • Free Tier: Manage up to 2 active streaming providers, add titles manually, and track your watched history with full offline support.
  • Streamwise Pro ($3.99/mo or $39.99/yr): Unlocks unlimited streaming providers, 1-tap Letterboxd sync, Renewal Radar cancellation shortcuts, full TV show season/episode tracking, curated film podcast recommendations, and background availability alerts. Try it free for 7 days!
  • New Availability Push Alerts: Proactive alerts in your notification shade whenever a title on your watchlist becomes available to stream on one of your active subscriptions without paying extra.

1. 🎯 ROI Command Center & The Churn Optimizer (Tab 2)

The ROI page is no longer just historical math—it's a forward-looking recommendation engine that tells you what to subscribe to next and what to cancel.

Two View Modes

  • Watchlist Match (Default): Ranks every streaming service by how many movies on your Watchlist are currently available to stream there right now.
  • Spend & Usage Historical monthly streaming burn rate, hours watched, cost-per-hour, and underutilized channels.

Strategic Opportunity Banners & Renewal Radar

  • 🚀 "Best Opportunity to Subscribe": Identifies the #1 inactive channel with the highest concentration of movies on your watchlist (e.g., "12 movies on your list are on Criterion Channel! Subscribe for 1 month at $10.99 ($0.91/movie) to binge them.") with a 1-tap Activate button.
  • 💡 "Safe to Pause" Alert: Highlights active services where you have 0 or 1 movie left on your list and low watch time, calculating your exact monthly savings.
  • ⚡ Renewal Radar & 1-Click Pause: Proactive countdown badges ("Renews in 3 days · Pause before next charge!") warn you before subscriptions auto-renew. Tap "Pause 1-Click (Official)" to immediately open the provider's official cancellation portal (Netflix, Max, Disney+, Hulu, Paramount+, Criterion, Apple TV+, Prime, Peacock).

Interactive Subscriptions & Price Presets

  • Quick Subscriptions Ribbon: 1-tap horizontal chip row to instantly toggle any service Active or Paused.
  • Subscription Edit Sheet: Tap any service or its gear icon to choose 1-tap price presets ($0 Free, $5.99, $7.99, $9.99, $13.99, $15.49, $19.99, $22.99).
  • Free Trial Tracker: Flip on trial mode with preset durations (7d, 14d, 30d, 3mo). Streamwise displays real-time countdown badges and prevents accidental pause alerts during active trials.
  • + Add Service: Add niche or custom channels with instant suggestions for Criterion, Peacock, Paramount+, MUBI, Shudder, BritBox, YouTube Premium, and F1 TV.
  • Direct Cancellation Links: Every active provider card features a 1-tap cancel icon taking you directly to the cancellation management page.
  • Watchlist Movie Chips: Tap any movie chip on a provider card to jump straight to that film's details or launch it on your TV!

2. 📺 Comprehensive TV Watch Options & Universal Cast

Streamwise connects directly with your living room hardware over your local Wi-Fi subnet with multiple reliable pathways:

  • Play on Smart TV / Fire TV: Always prominently available at the top of the Watch sheet with your TV name (e.g. Play on Danny's Fire TV). Streamwise communicates directly with the lightweight TvCompanionService running on your TV on port 8998. It acquires a screen wake-lock and immediately launches the official streaming app (Netflix, Disney+, Hulu, Prime Video, Tubi, Pluto TV, YouTube) with a search query for the movie, while automatically starting your watch session timer on the phone!
  • TV Connect Dialog: Tap the settings gear icon on the TV card or in Settings to scan your local Wi-Fi subnet. Live animation spinners and progress bars show active discovery, finding your Fire TV and Cast devices in milliseconds.
  • Watch on this Device: Deep-links directly into the official streaming provider app or mobile browser on your phone.
  • Cast or Open with App…: Opens Android's system chooser, allowing you to route playback via Google Cast / Chromecast or select another installed video player.
  • Already Watched (Log Now): Rapidly logs the title, star rating, runtime, and subscription attribution without starting playback.
  • Pin as Tonight's Feature: Pins the film to the top of your Watchlist banner for easy evening access.

3. 📋 Watchlist, Format Filtering & Cloud-Native AI (Tab 0)

A unified queue across movies, TV shows, and curated recommendations across all major streaming services.

  • Media Format Filtering: Seamlessly filter your queue between 🎬 Movies, 📺 TV Shows, and 🎙️ Podcast Recs with 1 tap.
  • Curated Film Podcast Feed: Discover standout films spotlighted on popular cinema podcasts like The Big Picture, The Rewatchables, and Blank Check, complete with quotes and host recommendations.
  • 1-Tap Letterboxd Import: Tap the Letterboxd chip or top-bar button to pull your entire public watchlist directly into Streamwise without needing an API key, or upload an exported CSV file. Streamwise automatically fetches streaming availability for each title.
  • Instant Undo on Deletion: Accidental deletions are completely reversible. Deleting any movie surfaces an immediate UNDO action in the bottom Snackbar and displays an inline banner at the top of your list so you can recover your film in 1 tap.
  • "Free to Me" Default: Shows only titles available on streaming services you currently have active (or free platforms), eliminating paywalls.
  • 🗓️ Release Radar: Never lose track of when favorite shows return or when movies become available. Tap the 🗓️ Release Radar filter chip to view upcoming season premiere dates (e.g. Severance S2E1, The Bear S4E1), digital SVOD / VOD drop windows, and theatrical releases with proactive notifications.
  • Duration Chips (<90m, <120m): Powered by exact TMDB movie runtime metadata. Tap < 90m for brisk evening watches or < 120m to filter out multi-hour epics. Displays live matching movie counts with a 1-tap "Clear all filters" shortcut.
  • Unified Quick Filters & Advanced Drawer: Filter chips now reside in a clean, horizontally scrolling single bar. Tap the list icon next to the sort dropdown to toggle the expandable Filter Drawer for fine-grained Service and Genre selections.
  • Top Bar Overflow Menu (⋮): Keeps the top bar clean and spacious—access the User Guide, Letterboxd Watchlist Import, and Letterboxd CSV Export via the convenient 3-dot overflow menu.
  • Olivia AI Agent & Quick Prompts: Powered by Google's Gemini 2.0 Flash for sub-second, highly contextual recommendations and streaming advice. Use 1-tap quick action chips ("What to watch tonight?", "Which subscription to cancel?", "When does Severance return?") for instantaneous guidance. Switch between Cloud Gemini and Local Ollama anytime in Settings.

4. ⭐ Frictionless Watched Logging & TV Episode Stepper (Tab 1)

Log what you watch with precision and keep your cinematic records portable.

  • TV Episode Check-In: For TV series, the Quick Log dialog includes season and episode steppers, auto-incrementing your progress and attributing episode hours to your subscription ROI.
  • 1-Tap Letterboxd Sync: Crawls public profile pages (letterboxd.com/username/watchlist/) to synchronize queued films with live streaming providers.
  • Letterboxd CSV Import & Export: Easily import watched.csv or watchlist.csv, and tap "Export to Letterboxd" to create a ready-to-import CSV file (Downloads/letterboxd_import.csv) matching Letterboxd's exact schema.
  • Quick Log Dialog: Rate movies from 0.5 to 5.0 stars, toggle the Rewatch flag, record exact runtime, and attribute the watch to the correct subscription.
  • The Vault: Your permanent viewing diary displaying personal star ratings, release years, runtime badges, and rewatch indicators.

5. ☁️ Master Google Sheet Cloud Ledger ($0/mo)

Your Watchlist and Watched history automatically sync with a shared Google Sheet via a dedicated serverless Google Apps Script webhook.

  • Pre-Configured: Built into the app with zero copy-pasting required.
  • Automated Podcast Ingestion: Scheduled background tasks analyze movie podcast episodes (e.g., The Big Picture, The Rewatchables) and automatically append recommended movies directly into your master sheet, which syncs back into Streamwise!

Streamwise User Guide

Welcome to Streamwise, your personal cinematic command center and streaming companion.

1. Watchlist & Discovery

  • Spotlight Carousel: Featured titles at the top of your watchlist with high-resolution TMDB posters, ratings, and genre tags.
  • Default View ("My Services"): By default, Streamwise filters your queue to only display movies and TV shows currently available on your subscribed streaming platforms and free-with-ads channels (Tubi, Pluto TV, Freevee).
  • Filter Chips: Tap "All" to view your complete wishlist regardless of availability, or select individual service chips (Netflix, Max, Hulu, Disney+, Prime Video, Criterion) to filter by a specific catalog.
  • Immediate Refresh: Tap the orange refresh icon (↺) in the top bar anytime to trigger an immediate live sync with TMDB.

2. Service Management & ROI Analytics

  • Active Subscriptions: Configure which services you pay for in Settings to ensure "My Services" reflects your real-world household subscriptions.
  • Cost Efficiency: The ROI Stats tab tracks your historical watch hours and calculates your effective hourly value per streaming subscription.

3. Metadata & Offline Resilience

  • Local SQLite / Room Vault: All titles, ratings, and notes are cached locally on your device for instant launch and offline access.
  • Background Updates: WorkManager checks provider availability every 4 hours in the background so your queue stays accurate.

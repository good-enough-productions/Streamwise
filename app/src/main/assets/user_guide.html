🎬 Streamwise User Guide

Streamwise is your intelligent streaming companion and subscription optimizer. It unifies your Watchlist across every service, routes playback directly to your Smart TV, logs your viewing history, and uses a forward-looking recommendation engine to save you money every month.

1. 🎯 ROI Command Center & The Churn Optimizer (Tab 2)

The ROI page is no longer just historical math—it's a forward-looking recommendation engine that tells you what to subscribe to next and what to cancel.

Two View Modes

  • Watchlist Match (Default): Ranks every streaming service by how many movies on your Watchlist are currently available to stream there right now.
  • Spend & Usage Historical monthly streaming burn rate, hours watched, cost-per-hour, and underutilized channels.

Strategic Opportunity Banners

  • 🚀 "Best Opportunity to Subscribe": Identifies the #1 inactive channel with the highest concentration of movies on your watchlist (e.g., "12 movies on your list are on Criterion Channel! Subscribe for 1 month at $10.99 ($0.91/movie) to binge them.") with a 1-tap Activate button.
  • 💡 "Safe to Pause" Alert: Highlights active services where you have 0 or 1 movie left on your list and low watch time, calculating your exact monthly savings.

Interactive Subscriptions & Price Presets

  • Quick Subscriptions Ribbon: 1-tap horizontal chip row to instantly toggle any service Active or Paused.
  • Subscription Edit Sheet: Tap any service or its gear icon to choose 1-tap price presets ($0 Free, $5.99, $7.99, $9.99, $13.99, $15.49, $19.99, $22.99).
  • Free Trial Tracker: Flip on trial mode with preset durations (7d, 14d, 30d, 3mo). Streamwise displays real-time countdown badges and prevents accidental pause alerts during active trials.
  • + Add Service: Add niche or custom channels with instant suggestions for Criterion, Peacock, Paramount+, MUBI, Shudder, BritBox, YouTube Premium, and F1 TV.
  • Watchlist Movie Chips: Tap any movie chip on a provider card to jump straight to that film's details or launch it on your TV!

2. 📺 Comprehensive TV Watch Options & Universal Cast

Streamwise connects directly with your living room hardware over your local Wi-Fi subnet with multiple reliable pathways:

  • Play on Smart TV / Fire TV: Always prominently available at the top of the Watch sheet with your TV name (e.g. Play on Danny's Fire TV). Streamwise uses a hybrid subnet port sweep that bypasses router multicast blocks to auto-discover your Fire TV in milliseconds. The TV Connect Dialog displays active scan animations and lets you pick any discovered TV or enter a custom IP with 1 tap.
  • Watch on this Device: Deep-links directly into the official streaming provider app or mobile browser on your phone.
  • Cast or Open with App…: Opens Android's system chooser, allowing you to route playback via Google Cast / Chromecast or select another installed video player.
  • Already Watched (Log Now): Rapidly logs the title, star rating, runtime, and subscription attribution without starting playback.
  • Pin as Tonight's Feature: Pins the film to the top of your Watchlist banner for easy evening access.

3. 📋 Watchlist, "Free to Me" Filtering & Instant Undo (Tab 0)

A unified queue across Netflix, Max, Hulu, Prime Video, Apple TV+, Criterion, and free ad-supported platforms (Tubi, Pluto, Freevee).

  • Instant Undo on Deletion: Accidental deletions are completely reversible. Deleting any movie surfaces an immediate UNDO action in the bottom Snackbar and displays an inline banner at the top of your list so you can recover your film in 1 tap.
  • "Free to Me" Filter: Shows only movies available on streaming services you currently have active (or free platforms), eliminating paywalls.
  • Duration Chips (<90m, <120m): Powered by exact TMDB movie runtime metadata. Tap < 90m for brisk evening watches (e.g. Before Sunset 80m, Malevolent 88m) or < 120m to filter out multi-hour epics. Displays live matching movie counts with a 1-tap "Clear all filters" shortcut.
  • Duration Sorting: Choose "Shortest Duration" in the Sort dropdown to arrange your queue from quickest to longest.
  • Olivia Agent Research: Every title includes synthesized cultural trivia, focus topics, and pre-watch notes generated by your local AI.

4. ⭐ Frictionless Watched Logging & Letterboxd Sync (Tab 1)

Log what you watch with precision and keep your cinematic records portable.

  • Quick Log Dialog: Rate movies from 0.5 to 5.0 stars, toggle the Rewatch flag, record exact runtime, and attribute the watch to the correct subscription.
  • The Vault: Your permanent viewing diary displaying personal star ratings, release years, runtime badges, and rewatch indicators.
  • Letterboxd CSV Export: Tap "Export to Letterboxd" to create a ready-to-import CSV file (Downloads/letterboxd_import.csv) matching Letterboxd's exact import format.

5. ☁️ Master Google Sheet Cloud Ledger ($0/mo)

Your Watchlist and Watched history automatically sync with a shared Google Sheet via a dedicated serverless Google Apps Script webhook.

  • Pre-Configured: Built into the app with zero copy-pasting required.
  • Automated Podcast Ingestion: Scheduled background tasks analyze movie podcast episodes (e.g., The Big Picture, The Rewatchables) and automatically append recommended movies directly into your master sheet, which syncs back into Streamwise!

6. 🐞 "Do It Now" Floating Feedback FAB

Encounter a bug or have an idea for a feature? Look for the floating feedback button on any screen.

  • Automatically captures a Compose screenshot preview.
  • Attaches device diagnostics (screen tab, active subscriptions, OS version).
  • Files an issue directly on GitHub labeled jules-triage for autonomous AI agent pickup and implementation!

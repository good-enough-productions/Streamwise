Streamwise User Guide

Welcome to Streamwise, your personal cinematic command center and streaming companion.

1. Watchlist & Discovery

  • Collapsible Spotlight: Tap the "Spotlight: Ready to Stream" header or chevron to collapse/expand the carousel, maximizing vertical screen real estate for your queue.
  • Default View ("My Services"): Streamwise defaults to filtering your queue by movies and TV shows ready to stream on your active subscriptions and free platforms.
  • Advanced Filters: Tap Filters on the home ribbon to open the filter sheet. Filter by specific providers, multiple genres, minimum rating thresholds (e.g. 7.5+), release era (2020s, Classic, etc.), and custom sort orders.
  • Immediate Refresh: Tap the orange refresh icon (↺) in the top bar anytime to trigger an immediate live sync with TMDB.

2. Cinephile Vault & Watch Diary

  • Vault Overview: View your total films watched, estimated screen time hours, average rating, and top 4 favorite genres.
  • Chronological Diary: Browse past watches organized by month and year (e.g., September 2026), complete with formatted watch dates (Watched Sep 4, 2026) and personal notes.
  • Grid vs. Timeline: Switch between the detailed diary timeline view and a dense visual poster wall grid using the view toggle icon.
  • 1-Tap Letterboxd Google Sheet Sync: Tap Sync to Google Sheet to export your full diary and watchlist directly into a cloud Google Sheet via Google Apps Script ($0/mo).

3. Service Management & ROI Analytics

  • Active Subscriptions: Configure which services you pay for in Settings to ensure "My Services" reflects your real-world household subscriptions.
  • Cost Efficiency: The ROI Stats tab tracks your historical watch hours and calculates your effective hourly value per streaming subscription.

4. Beta Feedback & Autonomous AI

  • Floating Feedback FAB: When enabled in Settings, a global feedback button lets you capture diagnostics and submit issues.
  • Jules AI Opt-In: Submitted feedback goes directly to the project backlog. Check Assign to Jules (Autonomous AI) when you want the AI developer to autonomously pick up and code a fix.

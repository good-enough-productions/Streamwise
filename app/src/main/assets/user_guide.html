Streamwise User Guide

Welcome to Streamwise, your personal cinematic command center and streaming companion.

1. Watchlist & Discovery

  • Live Title Counts: Keep track of your queue size at a glance via the Top App Bar (Watchlist • X Titles), Bottom Navigation badge, and the sticky summary bar (Showing X of Y titles).
  • Collapsible Spotlight: Tap the Spotlight: Ready to Stream header or chevron to toggle between the full 8-title carousel and a compact 36dp header bar. Collapsing Spotlight frees up 1/3 of your screen for immediate movie browsing, and your preference is remembered automatically.
  • Streamlined Filter Bar: Enjoy a clean, uncluttered homepage ribbon featuring [✓ My Services], [Free w/ Ads], and a dedicated [Filters] chip with an active count badge. When filters are active, a single [Clear (X) ✕] chip lets you reset with one tap.
  • Default View ("My Services"): Streamwise defaults to filtering your queue by movies and TV shows ready to stream on your active subscriptions and free platforms.
  • Podcasts & Media Mentions: Filter your watchlist to titles covered on your favorite film podcasts, including What Went Wrong, The Rewatchables, The Big Picture, Blank Check, Unspooled, and How Did This Get Made?. When a podcast filter is active, Streamwise automatically displays all covered movies across all providers so you never miss a recommendation. All imported recommendations are strictly validated against TMDB to ensure only feature films and series are queued. Toggle "Featured Discussion Only" to focus exclusively on dedicated deep-dives. Powered by an offline catalog of 2,300+ podcast-discussed titles and real-time Google Sheet synchronization.
  • Multi-Select Advanced Filters: Tap Filters on the home ribbon to open the filter sheet. Select multiple streaming platforms, genres, and release eras (2020s, Classic, etc.) to widen your search with OR logic. Active filters appear as 1-tap dismissible tags on the sticky ribbon.
  • Actor Age at Release (Cast Scroll): Tap any movie card to open Movie Details and explore the horizontal Cast & Age scrollbar. See each actor's character, profile photo, and exact age when the film hit theaters (e.g. 🎂 Age 38 at release) plus deceased indicators (🕊️) without leaving the app.

2. Cinephile Vault, Viewing Analytics & Letterboxd Sync

  • Streamlined Vault & Decade Trends: The Watched Vault features visual decade trend capsules (2000s (34%), 2010s (26%), 1990s (19%), 2020s (15%)) right in the header card. Every film in your vault displays its confirmed release year (Title (Year)), extracted star ratings, and watch date badge.
  • 1-Tap Letterboxd + Google Sheet Sync: Tap the prominent Sync Letterboxd & Google Sheet button on the Watched page to simultaneously pull your latest live Letterboxd diary entries and back up your vault to Google Sheets with live status feedback.
  • Cinephile Viewing Analytics Hub: Tap the Viewing Analytics & Eras banner or top analytics chart icon to open your personal cinephile dashboard:
    • Auteurs & Directors: Ranked film counts across top directors (Coen Brothers, Nolan, Scorsese, Fincher, Tarantino, Spielberg, etc.) with star ratings and sample title tags.
    • Stars & Screen Presence: Track appearances across leading actors (Tom Cruise, Matt Damon, Leonardo DiCaprio, Brad Pitt, Robert De Niro, etc.).
    • Cinephile Blind Spots & Watchlist Integration: Detects underrepresented areas in your vault (Pre-1970s Golden Age, 70s New Hollywood, Westerns, Documentaries) and lists matching films from your 1,100+ Watchlist queue ready to stream.
    • Cinema Eras & Decades Breakdown: Visual progress bars across 2020s down to Pre-1970s classic cinema with 0% unknown era and 1-tap filtering.
    • TMDB Rating Enrichment: 1-tap action to query TMDB and enrich unrated diary entries with TMDB scores and genres.
    • Rhythm & Velocity: Displays your rolling 6-month viewing velocity (e.g. ~7.0 films/mo) alongside your all-time peak viewing month.
  • "Service Used" Logging & Filtering: Tag the exact streaming service (Netflix, Max, Criterion, etc.) or theater you used to watch any film directly inside Movie Details. Filter your watched vault by service to see your viewing breakdown.
  • Local CSV & ZIP File Tools: Expand the CSV file tools drawer in the Watched Vault or Settings to import/export standard Letterboxd archives (Title,Year,Rating10,WatchedDate) with intelligent deduplication.

3. Explore & Cinematic Culture (4-Subtab Hub)

  • ✨ AI & Taste: Review your dynamic Cinephile Taste Matrix, read deep Gemini 3.1 Pro syntheses of your vault's core themes and auteur ties, add tailored recommendations to your watchlist in 1-tap, and trigger quick conversation starters.
  • 💬 Olivia AI: Dedicated fullscreen conversational cinema assistant with persistent badge counter. Discuss analyses, ask for curated double features, or debate film history.
  • 🎙️ Podcasts Hub: Browse episodes from What Went Wrong, The Rewatchables, The Big Picture, Blank Check, and How Did This Get Made?. Filter by show title, listen instantly, or tap Ask Olivia to discuss the episode.
  • ✨ Gemini Spark Podcast Tracker: 1-tap sync button in both Explore and Settings pulls new movie recommendations from your personal Google Sheet tracker. New titles are added to your Watchlist tagged with origin details (podcast name, episode, air date, recommender), and any previously seen movies in your Watched Vault are updated with discussion notes.
  • 📰 Film News Hub: Read live cinephile dispatches categorized into Box Office, Festivals, Auteurs, and Streaming, with 1-tap Discuss with Olivia integration.

4. Service Management, Tenure & Live Deals

  • Single-Service Rotation Advisor & Expected Value: Based on your real watch velocity (~7.0 films/mo), Streamwise calculates your exact effective cost per film and highlights how much you can save (up to ~$49/mo or ~$588/yr) by keeping only 1 subscription active at a time and rotating month-to-month. It automatically identifies the service with the largest backlog in your watchlist and calculates how many months of viewing are already queued.
  • Interactive Provider Cards & Watchlist Volume: Every card in My Services displays live counts of how many titles currently on your watchlist are available on that provider (e.g. 🎬 10 Watchlist Titles). Inactive services show queued counts so you know if resubscribing unlocks films you want to see.
  • Subscription Tenure & Value: Track how long you've subscribed to each service and review your effective cost per hour ($/hr) based on logged viewing time.
  • Service Watchlist Queue & Live Deals: Tap any provider card to inspect its details, view the exact watchlist movies available on that platform, and search live deals across The Streamable, Slickdeals, Doctor of Credit, and Google Deals.

5. OTA Updates & 4-Tab Settings Hub

  • Unified Hub: Modernized settings split into Profile & Letterboxd, Services, Guides, and System tabs.
  • In-App GitHub Updates: Streamwise checks GitHub Releases for new updates. Download and install new APK builds directly within the app without manual browser downloads.

6. Beta Feedback & Autonomous AI

  • Floating Feedback FAB: When enabled in Settings, a global feedback button lets you capture screenshots, diagnostics, and submit issues directly into GitHub backlog.
  • Multi-Tier Resilient Delivery: Feedback routes seamlessly through authenticated Cloud Run and Google Apps Script webhooks, with sticky error notices and Toast confirmations.
  • Jules AI Opt-In: Submitted feedback goes directly to the project backlog for review. Check Assign to Jules (Autonomous AI) when you want the AI developer to autonomously pick up and code a fix.

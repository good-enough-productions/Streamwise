Streamwise User Guide

Welcome to Streamwise! This app has two main goals: helping you find exactly what to watch, and making sure you aren't paying for subscriptions you aren't actually using.

1. The Watchlist (Your Queue)

What it is: A single place for all the movies and shows you want to watch.

No more jumping between Netflix, Hulu, and Max to remember what you saved. Search for any movie or show, and add it to your Watchlist. When you're ready to watch, the app tells you exactly which of your active services has it right now.

2. Searching & Adding

How to do it: Tap the Search tab at the bottom of the screen.

Type in a movie or show. When you see something you like, tap the "+" button to add it to your Watchlist. The app uses TMDB (The Movie Database) and Watchmode to automatically find out where it's streaming.

3. Managing Your Subscriptions (Subs Tab)

How to do it: Tap the Settings (Gear) icon, then go to the Subs tab.

Tell the app which streaming services you currently pay for. This is crucial for the Watchlist to accurately show you "My Services" vs everything else.

Custom Pricing & Trials: Tap on any active service to expand its details. If you're on a .99/mo trial, enter that price and the days until the trial ends. Streamwise will use this real data for your ROI calculations, and automatically disable the service when the trial expires!

4. ROI & Budget Tracking

What it is: Tap the ROI Stats tab to see if your subscriptions are worth the money.

Are you paying /mo for Max but only watched 1 hour this month? Streamwise tracks your "Watch Sessions" when you check off items from your Watchlist. It calculates a "Cost Per Hour" for each service. If a service costs too much per hour of entertainment, it gets flagged as a CANCEL CANDIDATE to save you money.

5. The AI Chatbot

What it is: Tap the AI Chat tab to talk to your local AI.

Not sure what to watch? Ask the AI! "Give me a 90s action movie available on Netflix." The AI talks to your local Ollama server to give you private, smart recommendations.

Tip: Keep this app updated with your true subscription costs to get the most accurate savings recommendations!

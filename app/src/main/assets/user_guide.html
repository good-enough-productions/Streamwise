Streamwise User Guide

Welcome to Streamwise, your personal cinematic command center and streaming companion.

1. Watchlist & Discovery

  • Live Title Counts: Keep track of your queue size at a glance via the Top App Bar (Watchlist • X Titles), Bottom Navigation badge, and the sticky summary bar (Showing X of Y titles).
  • Natural Scrolling Spotlight & Sticky Search: The "Spotlight: Ready to Stream" carousel scrolls away naturally as you scroll down your watchlist, while the Search bar and Filter ribbon stick firmly to the top for quick access. Scroll back to the top anytime to reveal Spotlight again.
  • Default View ("My Services"): Streamwise defaults to filtering your queue by movies and TV shows ready to stream on your active subscriptions and free platforms.
  • Podcasts & Media Mentions: Filter your watchlist to titles covered on your favorite film podcasts, including What Went Wrong, The Rewatchables, The Big Picture, Blank Check, and How Did This Get Made?. Toggle "Featured Discussion Only" to focus exclusively on dedicated deep-dives.
  • Multi-Select Advanced Filters: Tap Filters on the home ribbon to open the filter sheet. Select multiple streaming platforms, genres, and release eras (2020s, Classic, etc.) to widen your search with OR logic. Active filters appear as 1-tap dismissible tags on the sticky ribbon.

2. Cinephile Vault & Letterboxd Sync Pipeline

  • Live Letterboxd RSS Sync: Tap Sync RSS Diary to pull your recent diary entries from letterboxd.com. Streamwise parses titles, star ratings, and watch dates, adding missing films straight to your Watched Vault.
  • Local CSV & ZIP File Import: Tap Import CSV/ZIP (in Watched Vault or Settings) to import watchlist.csv, watched.csv, diary.csv, ratings.csv, or full account ZIP exports (letterboxd-*-data.zip) with intelligent deduplication.
  • Two-Way Export for Letterboxd: Tap Export for LB to generate a standard Letterboxd import CSV (Title,Year,Rating10,WatchedDate) via Android share sheet and upload directly to https://letterboxd.com/import/.
  • 1-Tap Web Actions: In Settings, open 1-tap links to Letterboxd watchlist export, account data export, and the web importer.
  • Vault Overview & Diary: View total films watched, screen time hours, average rating, top 4 genres, and chronological diary grouped by month/year with timeline and grid toggles.
  • Google Sheet Ledger Sync: Export your full diary and watchlist directly into a cloud Google Sheet via Google Apps Script ($0/mo).

3. Explore & Cinematic Culture (4-Subtab Hub)

  • ✨ AI & Taste: Review your dynamic Cinephile Taste Matrix, read deep Gemini 3.1 Pro syntheses of your vault's core themes and auteur ties, add tailored recommendations to your watchlist in 1-tap, and trigger quick conversation starters.
  • 💬 Olivia AI: Dedicated fullscreen conversational cinema assistant with persistent badge counter. Discuss analyses, ask for curated double features, or debate film history.
  • 🎙️ Podcasts Hub: Browse episodes from What Went Wrong, The Rewatchables, The Big Picture, Blank Check, and How Did This Get Made?. Filter by show title, listen instantly, or tap Ask Olivia to discuss the episode.
  • 📰 Film News Hub: Read live cinephile dispatches categorized into Box Office, Festivals, Auteurs, and Streaming, with 1-tap Discuss with Olivia integration.

4. Service Management, Tenure & Live Deals

  • Interactive Provider Cards: Tap any provider in My Services or the Other Streaming Services tray to inspect its details, monthly cost, plan tier, and renewal day.
  • Subscription Tenure & Value: Track how long you've subscribed to each service and review your effective cost per hour ($/hr) based on logged viewing time.
  • Live Deal Aggregators: Tap 1-tap shortcut buttons to search current deals, bundles, and student/military promos on The Streamable Deals, Slickdeals, Doctor of Credit, and Google Deals.

5. OTA Updates & 4-Tab Settings Hub

  • Unified Hub: Modernized settings split into Profile & Letterboxd, Services, Guides, and System tabs.
  • In-App GitHub Updates: Streamwise checks GitHub Releases for new updates. Download and install new APK builds directly within the app without manual browser downloads.

6. Beta Feedback & Autonomous AI

  • Floating Feedback FAB: When enabled in Settings, a global feedback button lets you capture diagnostics and submit issues.
  • Jules AI Opt-In: Submitted feedback goes directly to the project backlog. Check Assign to Jules (Autonomous AI) when you want the AI developer to autonomously pick up and code a fix.

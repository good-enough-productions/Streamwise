The 3-Horizon Flywheel: Intent Browsing → Streaming ROI → Film Culture Hub

Why Current Streaming UX Fails the User (The Friction)
  • Provider agenda over user intent: Netflix, Prime, and Max push content they've paid to license or high-margin originals, not what you actually care to watch.
  • Buried watchlist as an afterthought: Your personal watchlist is relegated to row 5 or 6 as an unsorted, unmanageable 80-item junk drawer.
  • Cognitive silo fatigue: You bounce between 4 apps, forgetting which service holds *Heat* or *Severance*, repeatedly getting hit with sign-in prompts.
The Proposed Streamwise 3-Horizon Flywheel
  • 1. The Hook (Browsing): Watchlist *is* the operating system. AI & semantic metadata cluster your queue into dynamic shelves (Brad Pitt, 90s Thrillers, Under 95m).
  • 2. The Retention (ROI & Rotation): Instantly filter by active subscriptions. "Reactivation Radar" flags inactive services when enough saved items justify a 30-day binge.
  • 3. The Moat (First Stop Culture): Cinema news, trailer alerts, and matching podcasts (*The Rewatchables*, *The Big Picture*) right next to your queue.
